Adaptations: combined torso and groin, legs and feet, and the tail and its
feathers. Integrated hand and foot plumage, split eyes by side, and centered
the detached tail sprite. Renamed states and adjusted grayscale for native
color multiplication while preserving the original poses and pixel positions.

The fitted damage sheet uses PrimeDream's icons/mob/effects/dam_mob.dmi for
wounds and burns, refitted to the bodypart silhouettes. Husk shading is
adapted from the SierraBay12 Resomi husk.dmi in the species source above.
