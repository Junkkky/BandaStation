Worn Resomi clothing adapted from SierraBay12 at
40edecd4203679a060f826f00ff773964582a4c2, whose Resomi module is based on Infinity.
https://github.com/SierraBay/SierraBay12/tree/40edecd4203679a060f826f00ff773964582a4c2/mods/resomi/icons/clothing

Changes: selected corresponding item sprites, renamed equivalent states for
PrimeDream, retained the first fitted version of repeated states, and mirrored
the headset for the left slot. Composited the source hardhat lamp effect for lit
states. Split the fitted jumpsuit and sneakers into the existing recolorable
layers. Fitted the native rolled uniform variants and welding visor to Resomi.
PrimeDream source for the latter states: icons/mob/clothing/under/color.dmi and
icons/mob/clothing/head/utility.dmi.

The fitted underwear sheet derives from PrimeDream's icons/mob/clothing/underwear.dmi.
It retains the selectable state names, palette pixels and directions. Existing
styles were fitted at the shoulders, waist and legs to follow the Resomi silhouette.

The additional fitted clothing uses PrimeDream worn artwork, including the
64-pixel shoes in icons/mob/large-worn-icons/64x64/feet.dmi. Other sources are
icons/mob/clothing, icons/obj/clothing/head/beret.dmi and the modular wintercoat
and hood sheets. The adapted states retain the native palettes and animations.

Further fitted states use native PrimeDream worn artwork for departmental
clothes, masks, glasses, spacesuits, neckwear, backpack equipment and suit
storage. Base sprites, active variants and overlay layers retain their original
state names and colors.
