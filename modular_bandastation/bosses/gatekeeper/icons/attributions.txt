gatekeeper.dmi
Gatekeeper design generated with OpenAI imagegen on 2026-11-06,
following Junkkky's references and art direction; pixel adaptation by Codex.
Design: pale organic ceremonial vestments, small head, elongated regal posture,
facial ritual markings, no pins or headdress. Four directions, eight idle frames.
Native canvas: 32x96 pixels, 24-color base with intermediate breathing shades,
binary transparency.
Adaptation: filtered proportional underlay, pixel retouch of the face and organic
plates, fringe removal, idle assembled from one fixed pose per direction.
BYOND state: idle; S/N/E/W, eight frames, 0.5 seconds per frame.
dead: static first idle pose in each direction, added for the playable prototype.
Breathing added on 2026-11-10: tapered chest light/shadow variation, maximum
6 RGB levels. No pixel displacement; head, shoulders, hands and robe stay fixed.
No pixels from the reference photographs or concept illustrations were copied.
