spider_clown.dmi
Design reference: four-view concept art of the spider clown supplied by Junkkky (event organizer).
Rebuilt on 2026-11-20 from that concept: the body (abdomen, face, ruff, fringe) is the concept itself,
reduced to the 96x96 canvas by averaging each block (slight contrast and saturation lift) and mapping it
to a 32-colour palette taken from that result; no outline.
The eight legs are drawn procedurally in the concept's colours (bone femurs, red claws) and animated
leg by leg; each frame is composed at 8x and pixelized with the concept's own colours. The only face pixels set by hand are the
two yellow eyes, placed where the concept has them.
idle: 6 frames, breathing (body rises and falls) and drool from the mouth.
idle (movement=1): 16-frame alternating-tetrapod walk.
bite: 5 frames, lunge towards the facing direction. stomp: 5 frames, rear up and slam (stomp, jump landing).
dead: the first idle pose turned over.
idle_eyes, bite_eyes, stomp_eyes: emissive layers of the concept's yellow eye pixels, frame for frame with their states.
Generator scripts kept outside the repository (Documents/Codex/GateClosure/spider_sprite).
The previous sprite and its attribution are archived outside the repository.

spider_clown_true.dmi
Design reference: four-view concept art of the clown's true form supplied by Junkkky (event organizer).
Made on 2026-11-20 from that concept: each view reduced to 64x96 by averaging blocks (slight contrast and
saturation lift) with a shared 32-colour palette taken from the result; feet stand on the bottom tile.
idle: 8 frames; the body above the waist (arms included) rises one pixel to breathe, tentacles sway by one pixel.
idle_eyes: emissive layer of the concept's yellow eye pixels, frame for frame. dead: the resting pose, still.
Generator kept outside the repository (Documents/Codex/GateClosure/clown_sprite).
